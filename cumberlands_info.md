# University of the Cumberlands Student Resources

Official University of the Cumberlands offices, services, and links used
by the chatbot. Each section starts with "## " so the chatbot can find it.

Source: official pages on https://www.ucumberlands.edu
Last checked: October 8, 2026. Verify before relying on this information.

## Current Students Hub
Help with: one page that links to student tools and services, including
Blackboard (iLearn), MyUC, university email, the Student Handbook,
tutoring, TimelyCare, and career services.
Link: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/current-students

## Assignment Deadlines and Course Information
Help with: assignment due dates, homework deadlines, course schedules,
syllabus, and course materials. Students find assignment due dates,
course announcements, and the syllabus in their course on Blackboard
(iLearn). For questions about a specific assignment, contact the course
instructor.
Link: https://ucumberlands.blackboard.com/

## Blackboard (iLearn) Learning Management System
Help with: logging in to online courses, course pages, assignments,
submitting homework, course materials, and Tutor.com online tutoring
(available inside Blackboard courses). iLearn is the university's name
for Blackboard.
Link: https://ucumberlands.blackboard.com/
iLearn help: ilearn@ucumberlands.edu, 877.633.9146

## MyUC Student Portal (Workday)
Help with: course registration, unofficial transcripts, tuition payments,
deferments, and applying for degree completion. MyUC runs on Workday.
Link: https://www.myworkday.com/ucumberlands/

## UCLogin and University Email
Help with: single sign-on to university apps (UCLogin) and university
email (Outlook webmail).
UCLogin: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/uclogin
Email: https://outlook.com/ucumberlands.edu

## Academic Calendar
Help with: semester start and end dates, term dates, breaks, holidays,
and university-wide academic deadlines. Course assignment due dates are
not on this calendar; they are in each course on Blackboard.
Link: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/academics/academic-calendar
Main university phone: (800) 343-1609

## Office of the Registrar
Help with: class registration, enrolling in courses, adding or dropping
courses, official transcripts (through Parchment), unofficial
transcripts (through MyUC), applying for graduation, diplomas, and
enrollment or degree verification.
Link: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/academics/registrar
Phone: 606.539.4316
Email: registrar@ucumberlands.edu

## Academic Advising and Student Success Coordinators
Help with: academic advising, choosing classes, course planning, degree
progress, and general academic questions. Student Success Coordinators
serve as academic advisors and work with faculty advisors for graduate
students. There are separate coordinators for graduate and undergraduate
students.
Link: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/academics/academic-advising-assistance

## Scholarships
Help with: university scholarships and external scholarships. Most
university scholarships are for full-time undergraduate students; online
students are not eligible for most of them. External scholarships can be
reported to financial aid.
Link: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/tuition-aid/types-financial-aid/scholarships
Email: financialaid@ucumberlands.edu

## Tuition Payments and Payment Plans
Help with: paying tuition and fees through the myUC Payment Portal,
payment plans, third-party sponsors, and account holds. Payments cannot
be made over the phone.
Link: https://www.ucumberlands.edu/student-life/student-services/student-financial-center/payments
Email: studentfinancial@ucumberlands.edu
